# Art Gallery Website — Montreal

Polished, responsive website for a small art gallery and exhibition space inside an artists' community building in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. About 1,000 sq. ft. of intimate, community-focused exhibition space.

## Pages

- Home — introduction and "Inquire About Booking" call to action
- The Space — 1,000 sq. ft. layout, floor plan, and practical details
- Amenities — what is included with a booking
- Exhibitions — featured show plus an archive of past shows
- Artist Bio — reusable template for exhibiting artists
- Booking & Contact — how to inquire or book, with an inquiry form
- About — the gallery and its artists' community building

## Notes

- All copy is in Canadian English.
- Gallery name, address, contact details, prices, and exhibition details are clearly marked placeholders.
- Photos are tasteful placeholders until real photos of the space are provided.
- The inquiry form prepares a copyable/downloadable summary and does not send automatically.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the repo to GitHub Pages / Vercel — it's a single self-contained static page.
